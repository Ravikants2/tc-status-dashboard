  [409] Camera Issue
  [410] Problems related to the physical camera device or its functionality.
  [411] Examples:
1) Camera not powering on
2) No video feed / black screen
3) Blurry, distorted, or frozen images
  [412] NAS/DVR/NVR Related Issue
  [413] Issues involving video recording, storage, or playback devices used with cameras.
  [414] Examples:
1) Recording not happening
2) HDD/SSD failure or not detected
3) Playback failure or corrupted recordings
4) Device not booting or frequently restarting
5) NVR/DVR offline or inaccessible
  [415] Internet Issue
  [416] Problems related to ISP connectivity affecting live streaming services.
  [417] Examples:
1) No internet connectivity
2) Frequent internet disconnection
ISP outage
3) Unable to access cameras live streaming
  [418] IP Related Issue
  [419] Issues caused due to incorrect or conflicting IP network configuration.
  [420] Examples:

1) IP address conflict
2) Incorrect subnet or gateway
3) Camera/NVR not reachable within LAN
4) Wrong static IP configuration
  [421] Proxy System Issue
  [422] Problems caused by proxy servers affecting application or network communication.
  [423] Examples:
1) Camera/NVR unable to connect to due to proxy
2) Proxy authentication failure(SOE)
3) Proxy not booting(HDD issue, Power issue)
